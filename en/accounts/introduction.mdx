Welcome to Cobo Accounts — the unified login and security verification management system designed for Cobo products, giving you full control over account security.

<Info>**Note**: Settings in Cobo Accounts are shared across both the development and production environments of Cobo Portal. Any changes to login or security verification methods will apply to both environments.</Info>

<img src="/cn/images/cobo-accounts/introduction1.png" className="screenshot_full_screen"/>

Cobo Accounts does not offer public registration. New accounts and organizations are provisioned with assistance from Cobo Sales. After receiving an activation email, users can configure their login methods and access Cobo products with a single set of credentials. For the request and activation process, see [Access Cobo Accounts](/en/accounts/sign-up). Cobo Accounts data is maintained in an independent enclave that follows stringent security and privacy standards.

## Key features

### Unified login system
Use a single set of login credentials to access most Cobo products after your account has been provisioned and activated.

### Flexible login methods
Use the login methods configured for your account, including email address, Google Account, Web3 wallet, and [Portal Mobile](/en/portal-mobile/introduction). Available methods depend on your account configuration.

### Diverse multi-factor authentication (MFA) methods
Currently, three MFA methods are supported: Authenticator App, Passkeys, and [Portal Mobile](/en/portal-mobile/introduction). Your MFA methods will be applied across most Cobo products by default, ensuring a consistent identity verification process and eliminating the need for repeated configurations.

### All-in-one account dashboard
View, add/remove, and modify your login and MFA methods easily through the Cobo Accounts dashboard. Gain a comprehensive overview of the services you use and the data stored in your Cobo Accounts.
