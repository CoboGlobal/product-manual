## Unified login system
Use a single set of login credentials to access most Cobo products in [Cobo Portal](/en/portal/introduction) after Cobo Sales has provisioned your account and organization.

## Flexible login methods
Use the login methods available to your provisioned account, including email address, Google Account, Web3 wallet, [Portal Mobile](/en/portal-mobile/introduction), and passkey. Available methods depend on your account configuration.

## Diverse multi-factor authentication (MFA) methods
Currently, three MFA methods are supported: Google Authenticator, security key, and [Portal Mobile](/en/portal-mobile/introduction). Your MFA methods will be applied across most Cobo products by default, ensuring a consistent identity verification process and eliminating the need for repeated configurations.

## All-in-one account dashboard
View, add/remove, and modify your login and MFA methods easily through the Cobo Accounts dashboard. Gain a comprehensive overview of the services you use and the data stored in your Cobo Accounts.
