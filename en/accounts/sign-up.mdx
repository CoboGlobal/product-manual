Cobo Accounts does not offer public registration. New accounts and organizations can only be provisioned with assistance from Cobo Sales.

## Request an account

1. [Contact Cobo Sales](https://www.cobo.com/book-demo/?utm_source=product-manual&utm_medium=product&utm_campaign=contact-sales).
2. Provide the required information, including your corporate email address, organization name, and business requirements.
3. Wait for Sales to create your account and organization.

## Activate and sign in

1. When you receive the activation email, follow its instructions to activate your account and configure a login method.
2. Go to [Cobo Accounts](https://accounts.cobo.com/) and sign in.
3. After signing in, configure multi-factor authentication. For details, see [Set up multi-factor authentication (MFA) methods](/en/accounts/set-up-mfa).

<Note>If you do not receive the activation email or the system says the account does not exist, contact Sales or [Cobo Support](mailto:help@cobo.com). The Cobo Accounts login page does not create an account for an unprovisioned email address.</Note>
