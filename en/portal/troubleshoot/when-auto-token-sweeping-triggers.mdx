**Applicable to: Custodial Wallets (Web3 Wallets), MPC Wallets**

Auto token sweeping has two layers: policy triggers and execution conditions. The available policy triggers, configured under [sweep policies](/en/portal/auto-sweep-policy-edit), are single transaction threshold sweep, balance sweep, and scheduled sweep. A scheduled policy records its daily execution time with a configured IANA time zone identifier.

Meeting a policy condition starts task creation and processing, but does not guarantee a completion time. The sweepable amount must still reach the wallet's configured minimum sweep amount, which is an independent security setting. The deposit address must also have sufficient gas or network fee available, either directly or through [Fee Station](/en/portal/fee-station/introduction).

If you need to trigger sweeping manually, create an auto sweep task through the WaaS 2.0 API. The task is still subject to its own `min_balance_threshold` and the applicable execution conditions. Refer to [Create auto sweep task](https://www.cobo.com/developers/v2/api-references/autosweep/create-auto-sweep-task).
