**Applicable to: MPC Wallets, Custodial Wallets (Web3 Wallets)**

If you see an insufficient funds error when initiating a transfer even though your token balance is sufficient, the most common cause is an insufficient balance of the native gas token used to pay on-chain fees. Token balances and gas token balances are different.

We recommend checking the following:

1. Check the Fee Station balance for Payment organizations.
    - For MPC Wallets and Custodial Wallets (Web3 Wallets), Payment organizations use [Fee Station](/en/portal/fee-station/introduction) for eligible gas-fee handling.
    - If the applicable Fee Station balance is too low to cover the fee, the gas fee cannot be paid from that balance and the transfer can fail with an insufficient-funds error even though your token balance is sufficient.

2. Check whether the gas-token balance is sufficient.
    - EVM networks usually require ETH (or the chain’s native token) to pay gas.
    - On TRON, fees are typically paid with TRX (or via related energy/bandwidth mechanisms).

If the issue persists, review the transfer details, confirm the wallet type, chain, token, and error message, and adjust the gas-token balance or applicable Fee Station balance before submitting the transfer again.
