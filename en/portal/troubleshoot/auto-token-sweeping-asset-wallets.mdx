**Applicable to: Custodial Wallets (Asset Wallets)**

Auto token sweeping for Custodial Wallets (Asset Wallets) is handled internally by Cobo. It automatically consolidates funds from deposit addresses into the designated address/wallet. You do not need to enable a switch or manage the sweeping process.

This internal consolidation is separate from the configurable [sweep policies](/en/portal/auto-sweep-policy-edit) available for MPC Wallets and Custodial Wallets (Web3 Wallets), including their [auto token sweeping enablement](/en/portal/enable-auto-sweep).

When Asset Wallet consolidation occurs is an internal process; the cost of withdrawing funds from an Asset Wallet is a separate fee topic.
