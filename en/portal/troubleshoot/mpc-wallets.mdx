## Auto token sweeping related issues

### Why can't some tokens be fully swept from the deposit address?  

See [Remaining balance after token sweeping](/en/portal/troubleshoot/remaining-balance-after-sweeping) for the network reserve and fee estimation conditions that can leave funds in a deposit address.

### How are gas fees paid for sweep transactions?

The deposit address must have enough gas for a sweep transaction, or [Fee Station can supplement the required gas](/en/portal/enable-auto-sweep).

### Can a deposit address initiate other transactions?

By default, a deposit address cannot initiate other transactions. If your workflow requires this capability, see [Enable deposit address transactions](/en/portal/auto-sweep-settings#enable-deposit-address-transactions).

### What conditions apply to a manually created sweep task?

A task created through [Create auto sweep task](https://www.cobo.com/developers/v2/api-references/autosweep/create-auto-sweep-task) is subject to its own threshold and the applicable execution conditions described in [When does auto token sweeping trigger?](/en/portal/troubleshoot/when-auto-token-sweeping-triggers).

### How can I view a wallet's sweep-to addresses?

Open the wallet's **Sweep-To** tab in Cobo Portal, or use [List sweep-to addresses](https://www.cobo.com/developers/v2/api-references/autosweep/list-sweep-to-addresses).

### What should I do if a sweep transaction is stuck or fails?

Follow [Auto-sweep transaction stuck or taking too long to confirm](/en/portal/troubleshoot/auto-sweep-transaction-stuck-or-unconfirmed) to review the task details and troubleshoot common causes.
