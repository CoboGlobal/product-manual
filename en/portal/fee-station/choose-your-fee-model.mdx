Choose a Fee Station configuration based on how your organization sends transactions. For funding mechanics, see [Fund flows](/en/portal/fee-station/fund-flows). To apply a configuration in Cobo Portal, see [Set up Fee Station](/en/portal/fee-station/set-up).

## Large child-address sweeps

Choose Fee Station for large sweeps when eligible child addresses need Network gas before their assets can be swept. Confirm that the wallet type supports Fee Station in the [support matrix](/en/portal/fee-station/introduction#supported-wallet-types) and maintain enough available Fee Station balance for the sweep. A sweep can fail when its Fee Station eligibility or quota check does not pass.

## High-frequency withdrawals

For frequent withdrawals from MPC Wallets or Custodial Wallets (Web3 Wallets), use Fee Station to reduce repeated gas funding work. Configure the payment preference for each chain under **Gas Token** > **Payment Preferences**. See [Configure payment preferences](/en/portal/fee-station/set-up#configure-payment-preferences) for the options currently available in Cobo Portal.

## Multichain operations

For operations across multiple chains, fund Fee Station with a supported USD stablecoin. Fee Station can use the shared balance for Network gas on [supported chains](/en/portal/fee-station/introduction#supported-chains-for-gas-fee-payment). The conversion uses an external quote plus a markup, as described in [Fund with USD stablecoins](/en/portal/fee-station/fund-flows#fund-with-usd-stablecoins).

Review the [supported token types](/en/portal/fee-station/introduction#supported-token-types) before choosing the funding asset.

## Occasional withdrawals

For infrequent withdrawals, configure only the EVM-compatible chains and TRON chains that your team uses. Manage each available choice under **Gas Token** > **Payment Preferences**, then follow [Configure payment preferences](/en/portal/fee-station/set-up#configure-payment-preferences) to save the configuration.

## TRON energy rental

For TRON transfers, Fee Station requests a live energy rental quote at the time of the transaction and applies a safety buffer to the quote. The cost is therefore dynamic rather than a fixed discount percentage.

Fee Station prioritizes Energy mode when energy can be applied and uses TRX burn mode otherwise. See [TRON fee payment](/en/portal/fee-station/set-up#tron-fee-payment) for how the two modes work.
