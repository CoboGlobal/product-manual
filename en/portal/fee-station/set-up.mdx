## Fund your Fee Station

### Deposit USD or stablecoins

1. Log in to [Cobo Portal](https://portal.cobo.com/login).
2. Click **Fee Station** in the left sidebar.
3. Open the **USD / USD stablecoin** tab.
4. Click **Deposit** next to **Total Value**.
5. Choose a deposit method and follow the on-screen instructions.

    <img src="/en/images/fee-station/deposit-u.png" className="screenshot_full_screen" alt="Deposit USD or USD stablecoins in Fee Station" />

### Deposit gas tokens

1. Log in to [Cobo Portal](https://portal.cobo.com/login).
2. Click **Fee Station** in the left sidebar.
3. Open the **Gas Token** tab.
4. Find the gas token for the target chain.
5. Click the <img src="/en/images/fee-station/icon_deposit.svg" className="icon" alt="Deposit icon" /> deposit icon and follow the on-screen instructions.

    <img src="/en/images/fee-station/deposit-gas-token.png" className="screenshot_full_screen" alt="Deposit a gas token in Fee Station" />

For help choosing a funding asset, see [Fund flows](/en/portal/fee-station/fund-flows).

## Choose a chain and token

1. Open the **Tokens** tab in **Fee Station**.
2. Click the <img src="/en/images/fee-station/icon_deposit.svg" className="icon" alt="Deposit icon" /> deposit icon next to the token, or click the deposit icon at the top of the page and select a token.

    <img src="/en/images/fee-station/2.png" className="screenshot_full_screen" alt="Choose a token to deposit into Fee Station" />

3. Copy the deposit address or scan its QR code.
4. To transfer from another wallet in Cobo Portal, click **Deposit from other wallets** and follow [Transfer assets](/en/portal/transfers/introduction).

## Configure payment preferences

1. Open **Gas Token** > **Payment Preferences**.
2. Expand the chain that you want to configure.
3. Choose one of the payment preferences currently displayed for that chain.
4. Click **Save Changes**.
5. Complete the security verification.

<img src="/en/images/fee-station/preferences.png" className="screenshot_full_screen" alt="Fee Station payment preferences" />

<img src="/en/images/fee-station/1.png" className="screenshot_full_screen" alt="Configure a chain payment preference" />

Configure preferences independently for the EVM-compatible chains and TRON options shown in Cobo Portal. See [Choose your Fee Station model](/en/portal/fee-station/choose-your-fee-model) for scenario guidance.

<Note>Whitelist-based fee configurations may apply to specific addresses. As a result, the fee behavior for those addresses can differ from the preference you set here.</Note>

## View your activity

In **Fee Station**, use the address icon to view a token's deposit address and the transaction-record icon to view its activity. Use the filter, sort, and search controls to locate tokens and records.

For API-based balance, address, and transaction reconciliation, see [Monitor Fee Station activity](/en/portal/fee-station/monitor-and-alerts).

## TRON fee payment

For TRON USDT transfers, Fee Station pays the on-chain fee in one of two ways:

- **Energy mode**: Fee Station uses available or rented energy on the sending address. Fee Station prioritizes this mode when energy can be applied.
- **TRX burn mode**: When energy cannot be applied, Fee Station pays the fee by burning TRX.

The displayed fee reflects the method used for the transfer. See [TRON energy rental](/en/portal/fee-station/choose-your-fee-model#tron-energy-rental) for how Fee Station determines the energy rental cost.
