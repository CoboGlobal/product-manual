## What is Fee Station

Fee Station is a team-shared account for paying fees across your organization. It is separate from the asset balance of any individual wallet. Any organization member can fund Fee Station, and the shared balance can pay for eligible wallets and fee types according to your organization's configuration.

Funding Fee Station does not move assets into a specific wallet. When an eligible transaction or bill occurs, the system checks whether Fee Station is configured to pay for that wallet or fee type. If it is eligible and the shared balance is sufficient, the payment is deducted from Fee Station instead of, or in addition to, the wallet's own balance.

Use [Fund flows](/en/portal/fee-station/fund-flows) to understand how assets enter and move through the shared account.

<Note>Currently, Fee Station supports paying gas fees for transactions made with MPC Wallets and Web3 Wallets on EVM-compatible chains, TRON, and Solana.</Note>

## Supported wallet types

Fee Station gas fee payment is available for MPC Wallets and Web3 Wallets only.

In the Cobo wallet taxonomy, Web3 Wallets are a subtype of Custodial Wallets. Custodial Wallets also include Asset Wallets, which are not eligible for Fee Station gas fee payment.

| Wallet type | Network gas |
| --- | --- |
| [MPC Wallets](/en/portal/mpc-wallets/introduction) | Supported |
| [Custodial Wallets (Web3 Wallets)](/en/portal/custodial-wallets/web3-wallets/introduction) | Supported |
| [Custodial Wallets (Asset Wallets)](/en/portal/custodial-wallets/asset-wallets/introduction) | Not supported |

## Who should use Fee Station

Fee Station is ideal for any team looking to simplify on-chain fee management, especially:

- **Exchanges and wallet service providers**: Centrally manage and prepay gas fees for end-user transactions, reducing operational overhead.
- **Payment service providers**: Use flat-rate or unified billing models to abstract away blockchain complexity and deliver smoother user experiences.
- **Enterprise developers using Cobo wallets**: Automate fee budgeting, track spending in real time, and receive Webhook alerts for transaction status and balance changes.

## What Fee Station can do for you

### Automatic gas fee handling

Fee Station can automatically cover gas fees for transactions initiated from MPC Wallets and Web3 Wallets. You can choose to cover fees only when the wallet balance is insufficient, or to always cover them. Once configured, the system automatically checks the condition and processes the gas fee without manual input. For more details about the chain support, please refer to [Supported chains for gas fee payment](#supported-chains-for-gas-fee-payment).

### How Auto Fueling works

To make sure a withdrawal address can pay its own on-chain fees, Fee Station may send a small amount of the native gas token to the address before processing the transaction. The cost of this top-up is deducted from your Fee Station balance. Depending on your configuration and available balances, the deduction may be settled from your USD or USD stablecoin balance even when a native gas token balance is present.

### Unified gas fee settlement

Your Fee Station balance settles Network gas across all supported wallet types and chains from a single balance. You don't need to maintain separate gas balances for each chain. For which wallet types are eligible, see [Supported wallet types](#supported-wallet-types).

### Flexible deposit options

You can deposit gas tokens directly, or simply deposit USD or USD stablecoins (such as USDT or USDC) as a unified balance. Fee Station will convert them into the required gas tokens at the time of transaction, avoiding the manual work of preparing tokens for each chain. For more details about supported gas token or stablecoin types, please refer to [Supported token types](#supported-token-types).

### TRON gas fee optimization with energy rental

Fee Station automatically leverages energy rental on the TRON network, prioritizing the use of energy to reduce the direct burning of TRX for gas fees. The entire process is fully automated, so you do not need to manage energy manually to benefit from fee optimization. 

Fee Station requests a live energy rental quote at the time of the transaction and applies a safety buffer to that quote, so the cost is dynamic rather than a fixed discount percentage. See [TRON energy rental](/en/portal/fee-station/choose-your-fee-model#tron-energy-rental) for details.

### API and webhook integration

Fee Station offers standardized APIs and webhook support, allowing your system to access real-time fee data and receive timely notifications. This makes monitoring and operations tracking easier and more efficient.

For more details, please refer to the [Fee Station API](https://www.cobo.com/developers/v2/api-references/feestation/get-fee-station-transaction-information) and [Webhook documentation](https://www.cobo.com/developers/v2/guides/webhooks-callbacks/webhook-event-type#webhook-event-types).

## Benefits of using Fee Station

The table below shows the differences between using Fee Station and not using it, helping you understand the benefits of using Fee Station:

The table below shows the differences between using Fee Station and not using it, helping you understand the benefits of using Fee Station:

| Category            | Without Fee Station                                    | With Fee Station                                         |
| ----------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Gas preparation**     | Manually prepare gas tokens for each individual blockchain | Deposit gas tokens or USD stablecoins once, and Fee Station will automatically cover gas fees. |
| **Gas fee strategy**    | Manual intervention required                               | Automatically applies configured rules, such as paying only when the wallet balance is insufficient, or always paying |
| **Fee settlement**      | Separate records across multiple chains and tokens         | All fees settled through a single account and in the same token, easier to track   |
| **Failed transactions** | High risk of failure due to insufficient gas               | Automatically detect and top up gas, significantly lower failure rate |
| **Maintenance cost**    | Frequent manual operations, time-consuming                 | One-time setup enables ongoing automation with less operational effort |
| **Data tracking**       | No unified record of fee activity                          | Exportable and well-organized records of deposits and spending |

By depositing fiat USD into your Fee Station account, you can cover multi-chain gas fees and platform charges without preparing gas tokens or pre-funding any crypto assets.

You can deposit USD stablecoins to cover the gas fees of multiple chains. No need to prepare gas tokens for each chain.
