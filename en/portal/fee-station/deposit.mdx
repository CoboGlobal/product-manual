<Note>You can deposit into Fee Station to automatically cover transaction fees. Gas fee payment currently applies to MPC Wallets (Organization-Controlled) and Custodial Wallets (Web3 Wallets) on the Cobo Portal web console for EVM-compatible chain and TRON transactions.</Note>

## Deposit USD or USD stablecoins

1. Log into [Cobo Portal](https://portal.cobo.com/login).
2. Click **Fee Station** in the left sidebar.
3. Go to **USD / USD Stablecoins** and click **Deposit** next to **Total Value**.
4. Choose a deposit method and follow the on-screen instructions.

## Deposit gas tokens

1. Log into [Cobo Portal](https://portal.cobo.com/login).
2. Click **Fee Station** in the left sidebar.
3. Go to **Gas Token** > **Tokens**.
4. Find the gas token for your target chain and click <img src="/en/images/fee-station/icon_deposit.svg" className="icon"></img> next to it.

    <Note>The **Deposit** icon appears only when the token supports deposits. If the icon is absent, the token does not currently accept deposits in your account.</Note>

5. In the pop-up window, copy the deposit address or scan the QR code.
    - To transfer from a wallet already on Cobo Portal, click **Deposit from other wallets** and follow the transfer instructions to complete a standard transfer to the Fee Station deposit address.

## Deposit TRON USDT into the stablecoin balance

To deposit TRON USDT into the Fee Station USD / USD stablecoin balance using an existing Cobo Portal wallet:

1. Click **Fee Station** in the left sidebar.
2. Go to **USD / USD Stablecoins** and click **Deposit** > **Deposit with Crypto**.
3. Select TRON USDT as the token and TRON as the network.
4. Copy the Fee Station deposit address shown.
5. To send from a wallet on Cobo Portal, click **Deposit from other wallets**. This prefills a standard transfer to the Fee Station deposit address. Follow the on-screen instructions to complete the transfer.

Use only the tokens, networks, and source wallets visible in Cobo Portal.
