Cobo Portal does not offer public registration or self-service organization creation. To create a new account and organization, contact Cobo Sales. After Sales provisions access, you can activate your account and sign in to Cobo Portal.

## Request access

1. [Contact Cobo Sales](https://www.cobo.com/book-demo/?utm_source=product-manual&utm_medium=product&utm_campaign=contact-sales).
2. Provide the required information, including your corporate email address, organization name, and business requirements.
3. Wait for Sales to confirm the solution and create your account and organization.
4. When you receive the activation email, follow its instructions to activate your account and configure your login and security verification methods.

<Note>If you have not received the activation email or need to change your organization information, contact Sales or [Cobo Support](mailto:help@cobo.com). Do not share passwords, email verification codes, or other login credentials.</Note>

## Sign in to Cobo Portal

After your account and organization have been provisioned, go to [Cobo Portal](https://portal.cobo.com/login) and sign in with your activated account. The Cobo Portal login page does not provide registration or organization creation.

## Choose a subscription plan

To purchase a paid plan for a provisioned organization:

1. Sign in to [Cobo Portal](https://portal.cobo.com/login).
2. Click the **plan badge** to the right of the organization name in the top navigation bar.
3. Select a plan and billing cycle, then follow the on-screen instructions to complete payment. For a customized plan, contact Sales.

<img src="/en/images/get-started/select-plan.png" className="screenshot_modal" alt="Select a plan"/>

## Notes

1. New accounts and organizations can only be provisioned with assistance from Cobo Sales. They cannot be created in Cobo Portal or Portal Mobile.
2. New users provisioned by Sales can use Portal Mobile if their accounts have never been bound to Cobo Guard.
3. Available plans and capabilities depend on your sales agreement and the status shown in Cobo Portal.
