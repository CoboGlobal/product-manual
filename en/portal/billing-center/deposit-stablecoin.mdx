## Deposit stablecoins

Use the stablecoin deposit flow when you need to add an available stablecoin balance in Billing Center. This flow is separate from [gas-token deposits](/en/portal/billing-center/deposit-gas-token).

To deposit a stablecoin:

1. Choose the available token.
2. Choose the network.
3. Use the displayed deposit address to transfer the stablecoin.

Stablecoin deposits and gas-token deposits serve different billing-related funding needs. Choose the flow that matches the asset you want to deposit.
