The **Overview** tab is built to answer one question at a glance: can your organization still pay, and if not, what runs out first?

## Balance

The **Balance** card shows your **Credits** balance. Hover **Payment scope** to see what it covers: transaction fees, monthly Cobo bills, and add-on plan quotas.

If you have turned on gas token top-up, a second **Gas Token** figure appears next to Credits, showing the USD value of every gas token you hold. **The two are shown side by side and are never added together** — they pay for different things, so a combined number would be misleading.

Use **Deposit** to add funds, and **Deposits** to jump to your deposit history.

### Low-balance alerts

Under the Credits figure is the alert threshold: **Notify me when the balance falls below** _[amount]_. Edit the amount inline and it saves immediately.

When Credits drop below it, Cobo emails your recipients and the Overview shows a **Top up Credits** prompt, which tells you the current balance, the threshold, and offers **adjust the alert threshold** if the number is simply set too high for how you operate.

<Note>
Setting a threshold to `0` turns the alert off for that balance.

A new organization starts with its thresholds at `0`, which means **low-balance alerts do not fire until you set a threshold yourself**. Set one on the Credits card, and on each chain row, as soon as you start funding the organization.
</Note>

You get an email when a balance falls below its threshold. While it stays below, Cobo keeps reminding you, but no more than **once every 24 hours** — so a balance left low sends one email per day, not one per transaction. Once the balance recovers above the threshold, the alert resets, and the next drop starts a fresh round.

Each balance is judged on its own, so Credits and several chains may each alert on the same day.

Configure who receives these emails in [Settings](/en/portal/billing-center/settings).

## Spent This Month

**Spent This Month** breaks your month-to-date spending into **Transaction Fees** and **Cobo Bills**. The two figures are read-only; the card's **Transactions** link opens [Transactions](/en/portal/billing-center/transactions) on the **Transaction Fees** group. To see Cobo Bills, switch groups once you are there.

## Upcoming Bill

**Upcoming Bill** shows the amount of your next Cobo bill, its **Charge date**, and the **Balance after charge**.

If Credits will not cover it, the card turns red and shows how much you are short: **Insufficient, short by** _[amount]_. If a bill is past due, the card shows **Overdue**.

<Warning>
Cobo does not retry an auto-charge on its own once it has failed. Topping up afterwards does not restart it — you have to pay the bill yourself from **Bills & Plans**.
</Warning>

## Gas Token Balance

This table appears only when gas token top-up is on. It lists the chains that hold gas tokens, each with its balance, USD value, and its own **Alert threshold** — editable inline, exactly like the Credits threshold.

A row is flagged **Below threshold** or **Above threshold**, and a row that is short shows how much: **_[amount] [symbol]_ short**, with a **Top up** action.

Narrow the table with the search box (**Search chain or token**) or the **Low balance only** filter.

<Warning>
Two behaviors are worth knowing before you rely on this table to decide where to top up.

**Chains with a zero balance are not listed.** A chain whose gas token is completely used up disappears from the table rather than showing as empty — even though it is the most urgent one. It reappears after you deposit to it again.

**Rows are ordered by USD value, largest first.** The chain holding the most value sits at the top and the one closest to running out sits at the bottom. Use **Low balance only** to surface the chains that actually need attention, rather than reading from the top of the list.
</Warning>

### When no chain holds gas tokens

If you have turned gas token top-up on but not yet deposited anything, the table explains the consequence, and the consequence depends on your deduction setting:

- With the default setting, transaction fees still come out of Credits, so nothing breaks.
- With **Gas tokens only** selected, Credits will not cover gas — so **transactions on every chain will fail**. See [Settings](/en/portal/billing-center/settings).

## Deposits that need your attention

**Deposits processing.** A deposit that has arrived but is not yet spendable is counted on the Balance card as _[n]_ **deposit(s) processing**. It becomes spendable once it clears; until then it is not part of your balance.

**Deposits failed.** Failed deposits are aggregated into a single banner — _[n]_ **deposit(s) failed** — with **View all** to open [Transactions](/en/portal/billing-center/transactions) filtered to them. Open a record to see its amount and status.

<Warning>Funds from a failed deposit are not credited, and they are not returned automatically. Contact help@cobo.com to resolve it.</Warning>

<Note>The failed-deposit banner counts **every** failed deposit in your organization's history, not just recent ones, and it cannot be dismissed. If it persists after support has resolved a case, it is most likely counting older records — open **View all** to see which.</Note>
