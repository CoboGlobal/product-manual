## Transaction records

Use transaction records to review Billing Center activity for a non-Payment organization. Records include deposits that add funds and payments that deduct funds for billing-related activity.

You can filter records and open a record to review its details. The available filters, detail fields, and table columns depend on the records shown in Billing Center.

## Export records

Users with the required permission can export Billing Center transaction records.

To export records:

1. Choose the record type.
2. Choose the date range.
3. Submit the export request.
4. Review the export status.

An export request can be generating, ready, or failed. You can create at most one export request per minute.

Exported records include grouped information about the organization, record identity, record type, asset, amount, transaction reference, and timestamps.
