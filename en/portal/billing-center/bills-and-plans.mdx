## Bills and plans

Use Billing Center to review billing and plan information for a non-Payment organization. Payment organizations continue to use [Fee Station](/en/portal/fee-station/introduction) for eligible fee management.

For existing documentation about pricing plan concepts, see [Pricing Plans](/en/portal/bills-and-payments/introduction). For existing documentation about bills, see [Manage bills](/en/portal/bills-and-payments/manage-bills).
