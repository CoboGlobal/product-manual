## What is Billing Center

Running an organization on Cobo Portal costs money in two different ways, and they behave differently.

Every on-chain transaction costs an on-chain transaction fee, and the token that pays it is usually not the token you are sending: sending USDC on Ethereum costs ETH; sending USDT on TRON draws on available energy and burns TRX when energy runs short. The token a chain charges its fees in is its **native token**. If a wallet address runs out of that chain's native token, its transactions fail. Separately, Cobo charges a monthly bill for your plan and any usage beyond it.

**Billing Center** is the single place where you fund both. You keep one prepaid balance, called **Credits**, and Cobo draws on-chain transaction fees and monthly bills from it. You do not have to hold each chain's native token yourself, and you do not have to reconcile fees by hand.

<Note>Fee sponsorship applies to transactions made with MPC Wallets and Web3 Wallets on EVM-compatible chains, TRON, Solana, and Tempo.</Note>

## Credits and gas tokens

Billing Center holds two kinds of balance. The difference between them is what each can pay for.

| | Credits | Gas tokens |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | Prepaid balance you top up with USDT, USDC, or USD, counted 1:1 in USD | The token a chain charges its fees in — its native token on most chains — deposited per chain |
| Pays for | Transaction fees on any supported chain, monthly Cobo bills, and add-on plan quotas | Transaction fees on its own chain only |
| Cannot pay for | — | Cobo bills |

Credits alone are enough to run an organization. Gas tokens are optional: you deposit them only if you want a particular chain's fees drawn from that chain's own native token rather than from Credits.

Because gas tokens are optional, **gas token top-up is turned off by default**. While it is off, Billing Center shows a single Credits balance and nothing chain-specific — no per-chain deposit entry and no gas token balance table. See [Settings](/en/portal/billing-center/settings) for how to turn it on.

## How the four tabs are organized

| Tab | What you do there |
|---|---|
| [Overview](/en/portal/billing-center/overview) | Read your balances, see what you spent this month and what the next bill will take, and set the balance at which Cobo warns you |
| [Transactions](/en/portal/billing-center/transactions) | Trace every fee, bill, refund, and deposit; filter, inspect, and export them for reconciliation |
| [Bills & Plans](/en/portal/billing-center/bills-and-plans) | Review your plan and resource usage, and pay or download bills |
| [Settings](/en/portal/billing-center/settings) | Turn on gas token top-up, choose per-chain sponsorship rules, and manage who receives billing emails |

## Before you start

To open Billing Center, log in to [Cobo Portal](https://portal.cobo.com/login) and click **Billing Center** in the left navigation bar.

Reading Billing Center and changing its settings are separate permissions. If you can see a balance but a control is unavailable, ask an organization admin to grant you the **Edit Fee Station Settings** permission. Bills and invoices are restricted further — only certain roles can see them.
