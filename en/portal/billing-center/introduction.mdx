## What is Billing Center

Billing Center is the billing-related management area for non-Payment organizations in Cobo Portal. Payment organizations continue to use [Fee Station](/en/portal/fee-station/introduction) for eligible fee management.

Use Billing Center to review billing-related balances, transaction records, bills and plans, and settings for your organization. The Billing Center documentation is organized into these topics:

- [Overview](/en/portal/billing-center/overview): Review billing-related balance concepts and find the related deposit and settings pages.
- [Transaction records](/en/portal/billing-center/transaction-records): Review deposit and payment records, and export records when you have the required permissions.
- [Bills and plans](/en/portal/billing-center/bills-and-plans): Review where billing and plan information is managed, with links to the existing bills and pricing plan documentation.
- [Settings](/en/portal/billing-center/settings): Review billing-related configuration.
- [Deposit gas tokens](/en/portal/billing-center/deposit-gas-token): Learn how the gas-token deposit flow is used for eligible gas-fee handling.
- [Deposit stablecoins](/en/portal/billing-center/deposit-stablecoin): Learn how the stablecoin deposit flow differs from gas-token deposits.
