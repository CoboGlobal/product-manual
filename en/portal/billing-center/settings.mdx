The **Settings** tab holds two things: **Advanced settings**, which control how transaction fees get paid, and **Email notifications**, which control who hears about it.

Every change here **saves immediately** — there is no save button.

Changes to Billing Center settings require MFA — this covers Advanced settings, the notification recipient list, and the alert thresholds you edit on the Overview.

<Note>Changing these settings requires the **Edit Fee Station Settings** permission. Without it you can still open the page and read your configuration, but Cobo blocks the change with a permission notice when you try to save one.</Note>

## Advanced settings

The summary line tells you where you stand without expanding anything:

- *Using the default setup: transaction fees are sponsored by Credits* — nothing chain-specific is in play.
- *Gas token top-up is enabled · sponsorship rules and deduction order are customizable*

### Gas token top-up

This is the switch that decides how much of Billing Center you see. It is **off by default**.

**While off**, transaction fees are paid entirely with Credits. There is no per-chain deposit entry and no gas token balance table — one balance, nothing to configure.

**Once on**, you can deposit native tokens such as ETH, TRX, or SOL per chain. On-chain transaction fees then draw on that chain's native token first; if that balance is not enough, Credits pay the entire fee instead.

<Warning>
You cannot turn gas token top-up back off while you still hold gas tokens. Cobo blocks it and tells you how much is left: *You still hold [amount] in gas tokens, so this cannot be turned off yet.*

Spend the balances down first. This is deliberate — turning the switch off hides all gas token UI, and Cobo will not hide a balance that is still being spent.
</Warning>

### Per-chain sponsorship rules

<Note>This section and the next one appear only after you enable **gas token top-up**.</Note>

<Warning>
**This section and the next one apply only to transactions initiated through the API**, and only when the request sets `auto_fuel` to `UsePortalPreference`. Both sections are tagged "API transactions only" in the interface.

**Transfers initiated from the Portal are not affected by these settings.** Their behaviour is fixed per chain:

| Chain | Fixed behaviour for Portal transfers |
|---|---|
| EVM-compatible chains | Use the gas tokens in the wallet address if they are enough; otherwise Billing Center pays the whole fee |
| TRON, Solana, Tempo | Sponsored by the Billing Center directly |

To stop Billing Center from covering a particular Portal transfer, choose that option in the transfer dialog. It is **available only on TRON, Solana and Tempo** — EVM-compatible chains do not offer it.
</Warning>

This section decides **whether Billing Center covers the fee when the native token in a wallet address is not enough**. Chains are grouped, and each group is set independently:

| Chain group | Options |
|---|---|
| **EVM, TON, APT and LAT chains** | No sponsorship · Sponsor when the address balance is short · Always sponsored by the Billing Center |
| **TRON** | No sponsorship · Sponsored by the Billing Center |
| **Solana** | No sponsorship · Sponsored by the Billing Center |
| **Tempo** | No sponsorship · Sponsored by the Billing Center |

- **No sponsorship** — pay the fee only with the native token the wallet address holds itself; **transactions fail when it runs out**. On TRON this means the address's own energy or TRX.
- **Sponsor when the address balance is short** — recommended. Use the address's own native token first; Billing Center covers the full amount when it runs short.
- **Always sponsored by the Billing Center** — never touch the address's native token; Billing Center pays the whole fee.

A chain group your organization does not support is not shown.

<Note>The **Solana** rule can be changed only by organizations on Cobo's newer fee model. If the Solana option is present but not editable, that is why — contact Cobo Support to confirm your organization's setup.</Note>

### Which balance to charge when sponsoring

<Note>As with the previous section, this choice applies to API transactions only. When gas tokens run short on a Portal transfer, **Credits pay the entire fee**.</Note>

Once sponsorship applies, this decides where the money comes from:

- **Gas tokens first, Credits when short** *(recommended)* — charge the gas token balance first; if it is not enough, Credits automatically pay the entire fee instead.
- **Gas tokens only** — charge only the gas token balance and never touch Credits.

<Warning>
**Gas tokens only** changes what a shortfall means. Instead of Credits quietly taking over, **API transactions fail outright** the moment a chain's gas token runs out, and you must deposit per chain to recover.

Cobo asks you to confirm before switching, and the change **takes effect immediately on every chain with sponsorship enabled** — not just one.

Choose this only if you want the two balances kept separate on purpose. If you do, treat the [Overview](/en/portal/billing-center/overview) gas token table and its per-chain alert thresholds as operationally critical: they are your only warning before transactions start failing.
</Warning>

## Email notifications

Billing notifications go to the recipient list you manage here. **Low balance alerts and bill notifications share one list** — there is no separate list per notification type.

| Notification | Sent when |
|---|---|
| **Low balance alert** | A balance falls below its threshold |
| **Bill notifications** | A Cobo bill is generated, 3 days before it is due, and when it is overdue |

Bill notifications are **always on** and cannot be turned off.

### Managing recipients

Click **Manage recipients**. Recipients are chosen from your **organization's members** — you pick a member, not an arbitrary email address. Add or remove them, then click **Done**.

<Warning>
The recipient list can be empty, and Cobo does not fill it in for you. When it is empty the page warns *Notifications cannot be delivered: Please add a recipient* — and **no low-balance alert or bill notice reaches anyone**.

Add at least one recipient when you set up your organization, before you rely on alerts.
</Warning>

<Note>Adding recipients requires permission to view organization members. Without it, the dialog explains that recipients cannot be added.</Note>

## Setting alert thresholds

Thresholds are **not** on this page. Each one sits next to the balance it guards, on the [Overview](/en/portal/billing-center/overview): the Credits threshold under the Credits figure, and each chain's threshold in its row of the gas token table.

<Note>A new organization's thresholds start at `0`, which means alerts are off. Set them yourself — see [Overview](/en/portal/billing-center/overview).</Note>
