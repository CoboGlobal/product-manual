## Deposit gas tokens

Use the gas-token deposit flow when you need to add a native gas token for eligible gas-fee handling in Billing Center. This flow is separate from [stablecoin deposits](/en/portal/billing-center/deposit-stablecoin).

To deposit a gas token:

1. Choose the applicable chain.
2. Choose the gas token.
3. Use the displayed deposit address to transfer the token.
